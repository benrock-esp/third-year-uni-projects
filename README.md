Schrodinger solver and eigenfunction finder
This script solves the time-independent Schrödinger equation for various potentials and explores bound states and wavefunctions in quantum mechanics, focusing on the harmonic oscillator and quartic potentials. The code tackles a series of computational tasks, including solving differential equations, finding energy eigenvalues, and visualizing wavefunctions. By implementing techniques such as the shooting method and bisection algorithm, I analyzed the behavior of quantum systems under different conditions.

ODE solver
This script solves ordinary differential equations and computes eigenvalues and eigenfunctions for a non-uniform string’s oscillations. I implemented Euler, Adams-Bashforth 2 (AB2), and Runge-Kutta 4 (RK4) methods to integrate a first-order ODE, analyzing stability and accuracy. Using RK4 with the shooting method and false position algorithm, I computed eigenvalues for a second-order ODE.
