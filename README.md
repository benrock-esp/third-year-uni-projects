Quantum Mechanics Numerical Solver

This project is a Python-based numerical solver for quantum mechanical systems, specifically designed to solve the time-independent Schrödinger equation for various potentials. It was developed as part of an academic exercise to explore bound states and wavefunctions in quantum mechanics, focusing on both harmonic oscillator and quartic potentials. The project demonstrates proficiency in numerical methods, scientific computing, and data visualization, showcasing my ability to implement complex algorithms to solve real-world physics problems.

The code tackles a series of computational tasks, including solving differential equations, finding energy eigenvalues, and visualizing wavefunctions. By implementing techniques such as the shooting method and bisection algorithm, I analyzed the behavior of quantum systems under different conditions, ensuring high numerical accuracy and robust results.
