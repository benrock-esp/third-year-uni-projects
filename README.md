Schrodinger solver and eigenfunction finder

This script solves the time-independent Schrödinger equation for various potentials and explores bound states and wavefunctions in quantum mechanics, focusing on the harmonic oscillator and quartic potentials. The code tackles a series of computational tasks, including solving differential equations, finding energy eigenvalues, and visualizing wavefunctions. By implementing techniques such as the shooting method and bisection algorithm, I analyzed the behavior of quantum systems under different conditions, ensuring high numerical accuracy and robust results.
